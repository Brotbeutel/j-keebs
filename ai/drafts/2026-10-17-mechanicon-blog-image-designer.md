# Mechanicon blog image — 2026-10-17 — status: draft

## Goal

Create a more distinctive 16:9 blog image for the Mechanicon announcement that connects the event with J-Keebs' attendance and fits the site's warm workshop-notebook identity.

## User journey

Visitors see the image first as a small teaser on the blog index, then at a larger size in the article. The image should identify both brands and the keyboard subject without repeating the article headline.

## Candidate

- Prototype image: `design/Mechanicon_2026-v2.jpg` (1920 × 1080, JPEG, 52.2 KB).
- Existing article image: `images/Mechanicon_2026.jpg` remains unchanged and in use until owner approval.
- Inputs: the supplied repository assets `images/mechanicon_logo.png` and `images/J-Keebs-Logo.png`; no external assets or requests.
- The Mechanicon mark is rendered at 336 × 336 px (1.49× its 225 × 225 source); the original logo file is untouched.

## Art direction

- Divide the canvas vertically into cream `#f4f0e1` and charcoal `#3a3736`, with a narrow warm-gold separator `#b3a078`.
- Make the Mechanicon mark the main focal point on the cream half at 336 × 336 px (1.49× its 225 px source), preserving its proportions.
- Place the date `17.10.2026` below the Mechanicon mark in dark Courier New bold (82 px).
- Keep the J-Keebs logo smaller on the charcoal half; place the supporting line `J-Keebs ist dabei` beneath it in Georgia (62 px).
- Align both logos on a shared visual centerline and both text baselines beneath matching fine rules. Georgia gives the headline an editorial serif feel; Courier New makes the date read as a precise event detail.
- Keep the layout restrained: no decorative keycap/color-swatch row and no circular outlines.
- Include no venue imagery, people, or claims about a booth or exhibitor status.

## Acceptance criteria

- At 1920 × 1080 and when scaled to the blog teaser, the Mechanicon mark and date are the first elements noticed; both remain recognizable.
- Neither logo is distorted; the Mechanicon logo stays within the 1.5× source-size limit.
- The split background fits the adjacent blog cards without a decorative color/keycap palette.
- No unsupported event information or extra text appears in the image.
- The current production image is replaced only after the owner approves this draft.
